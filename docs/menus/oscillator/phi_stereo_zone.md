# Phi Stereo Zone

Zone-style stereo control for the phi-family oscillators. Position within each of the eight zones sets the stereo AMOUNT (every zone starts mono, so the knob's origin is always mono); the zone selects the stereo CHARACTER, interpreted per oscillator — PhiWeave/PhiStair: dual scan taps (two pickup positions; zones vary tap distance, tonal tilt via a darker mip, counter-scanning); PhiGendy: dual polygon taps, with "lag" zones reading the right tap from the previous tick (~3ms micro-slap); PhiSwarm: slave separation (mid/side), with "flip" zones opposing the beat-AM between channels so injection pulling becomes autopan. Zones: Slim, Near, Open, Tilt, Wide, Sway, Split, Vast. Works with unison off and composes with unison stereo spread.

Available for PhiMorph, PhiStair, PhiWeave, PhiSwarm, and PhiGendy (PhiVox is excluded). PhiMorph currently renders dual-mono under the knob.
